# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Adults in Navi Mumbai (and surrounding areas) considering dental treatment — implants, smile design, orthodontics, laser procedures, and general care. They are selecting a specialist, not just a service: they are comparing practices, reading the doctor's credentials, and deciding whether this place feels trustworthy and calm. They may arrive anxious about dentistry in general. The site is often their first and decisive touchpoint with the practice.

Secondary: parents bringing children for paediatric care, and adults seeking the kind of unhurried, personalised experience that distinguishes a boutique studio from a volume clinic.

## Product Purpose

Bio Lume is a contemporary dental studio founded by Dr. Dishani Jain (BDS, Implantologist and Laser Specialist). The site exists to do one thing: earn the appointment. It must communicate that this practice is serious, precise, and calm — the antithesis of the anxious, clinical visit patients are trying to avoid. Every section should lower the activation energy to booking.

Success: a new visitor reads the page and books an appointment without needing to call first to verify the practice is real.

## Brand Personality

Quiet confidence. Considered. Unhurried.

Three words: **precise, warm, restrained.**

Emotionally, the site should feel the way a well-designed waiting room feels — good light, no noise, everything in its right place. Not cold. Not performative. The confidence of a specialist who doesn't need to shout.

## Anti-references

- **Clinical/hospital sterile:** white walls, blue cross icons, stock photo smiles, Comic Sans pricing tables, clip-art teeth. This is what Bio Lume is explicitly not. The site should feel nothing like a NHS dentist or a high-street chain.
- **Luxury spa cliché:** over-kerned italic headlines, marble texture backgrounds, gold foil, "bespoke wellness journey" copy, diffused candle photography. Calm and premium, yes — but the confidence comes from craft and evidence, not from aspirational lifestyle photography.
- **SaaS startup templates:** hero metric cards ("2,000+ smiles"), gradient text, identical icon-grid feature lists, glowing product screenshots. No startup energy here.
- **Heavy animation showreel:** excessive parallax, scroll-jacking, everything moving at once. Motion should be purposeful and rare — reserved for entrances, not for decorating every element.

## Design Principles

1. **Do less, more carefully.** Every treatment follows this; the site should too. Fewer elements, chosen precisely. Whitespace is not emptiness — it is confidence.
2. **Show, don't announce.** Technology is mentioned quietly ("tools we'd rather use than mention"). Copy earns authority through specificity, not adjectives.
3. **The visit starts here.** The site is the waiting room. Pacing, spacing, and tone should slow the experience down — not hustle the user toward a form.
4. **Visible quality.** Like Class-B sterilisation sealed in packaging, design quality should be something the viewer can sense without being told about it. Typography tightness, image selection, motion timing.
5. **Expert confidence, not expert distance.** Warm and precise together. The doctor treats adults like adults; the site should feel the same.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.1 AA. The site already respects `prefers-reduced-motion`. Maintain minimum 4.5:1 contrast on all body text and 3:1 on large text and UI components. Keyboard navigation for all interactive elements. Screen reader support via semantic HTML and appropriate ARIA labels where needed.
